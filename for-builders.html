Founders & Builders

Real work. Real experience. Lasting connections.

Build experience, grow your network through real work, and earn feedback and approved portfolio credit. Find paid opportunities or choose a voluntary contribution that fits your goals.

Start with your skills

Create your builder profile in the signed-in workspace. Add your strengths, learning goals and time available. Discovery is optional.

Find a manageable first contribution

Review the outcome, support, time and compensation before applying. Matches show why a contribution fits and which skills still need discussion.

Agree before you start

Confirm the exact scope and boundaries. Ask questions in the contribution conversation. An invitation or application does not mean you have agreed to work.

Show what you actually contributed

Deliver the work, exchange feedback, and use only separately approved portfolio text. Private reviews and unapproved work remain private.

Paid or voluntary. Clear before you start.

Founders and builders agree compensation, payment timing, ownership and scope directly. Founders & Builders takes no transaction commission and does not collect, hold, process or guarantee payment or resolve payment disputes. Feedback and portfolio credit do not replace an agreed payment.

Bring your agent, keep your say.

Your agent can help find opportunities and carry out supported actions within the permissions, limits and expiry you choose. It runs in your chosen app. Acceptance of agreements, reviews and portfolio sharing remain your decisions.
