Founders & Builders

Direction, support, and people to build with.

Turn your idea into a practical next step. Find people who share your vision, bring complementary skills, and help you move forward.

Start with the outcome

Describe what you are building, who it helps, and the next milestone. Your project overview stays private unless you choose to share it with approved beta members.

Make the first contribution clear

Agree on the deliverable, time, support, compensation, ownership and feedback. A small first contribution lets both people evaluate working together.

Find the fit

Matches explain shared skills, contribution time and compensation preferences. Discuss gaps and availability together; a match is not a commitment.

Build, reflect, repeat

Review delivered work, give specific feedback, and invite feedback on your own support. Approve portfolio credit separately from compensation.

Paid or voluntary. Clear before you start.

Founders and builders agree compensation, payment timing, ownership and scope directly. Founders & Builders takes no transaction commission and does not collect, hold, process or guarantee payment or resolve payment disputes. Feedback and portfolio credit do not replace an agreed payment.

Bring your agent, keep your say.

Your agent can help find opportunities and carry out supported actions within the permissions, limits and expiry you choose. It runs in your chosen app. Acceptance of agreements, reviews and portfolio sharing remain your decisions.
