Follow one task from a first conversation to useful feedback and a work story you can share. For anyone growing their skills and experience, and founders ready to support them.

Compensation is arranged directly. Project owners are responsible for their offers. Founders and contributors agree compensation directly. Founders & Builders does not set rates, process payments, guarantee payment, or resolve payment disputes. Feedback and portfolio credit are separate from compensation.

Fictional walkthrough: review one sample sign-up screen. These steps explain the tools; opening this guide creates no records or opportunities.

1. Offer a task2. Express interest3. Propose terms4. Confirm together5. Give feedback6. Record credit

01 · Founder

Offer one useful, manageable task.

A fictional founder needs three usability observations on a sample sign-up screen. The brief sets a 60-minute limit, what done means, a check-in, feedback within three days, compensation, ownership, and anonymized portfolio credit.

Before sharing: make the scope public-safe and resolve what support and compensation you can offer. A prepared listing is not proof of publication.

Prepare a small task →

02 · Contributor

Introduce what you can bring.

Discover a suitable small task, check availability with its owner, and create a notebook record. Add your chosen name, what you want to practice, availability, and questions. Preview the interest note, then download the note file or copy its text.

Share the note file or copied text directly with the intended founder. Copying or saving does not send it or reserve a place. If discovery is unavailable, discuss a task directly and record the agreed context.

Open your contribution notebook →

03 · Founder

Clarify fit, then propose exact terms.

Import the shared interest note file into the review desk, or paste its text, then open it for review. Ask about anything unclear. Use the task snapshot to start the scope, check every term, and add ownership terms. Download an agreement note file for the contributor, or copy its text.

Share the agreement note directly. A saved stage or drafted proposal does not mean the contributor has accepted. Resolve compensation before anyone starts.

Review contributor interest →

04 · Contributor

Review together before you begin.

Open the matching notebook record, import the founder’s agreement note file or paste its text, and choose Review received note. Check the task, project, chosen name, deliverable, time limit, compensation, support, ownership, and portfolio terms. Draft questions or a confirmation.

Send your questions or confirmation directly and get a shared understanding. Adding a proposal to your notebook does not sign it. Stop at the time limit and agree any extension.

Review the proposed agreement →

05 · Both

Deliver, discuss, and recognize the work.

The contributor shares three observations and asks for feedback. The founder reviews the actual work, describes what helped, suggests one next step, and agrees the chosen credit name and what may be shown. Then the founder downloads a feedback note file or copies its text and shares it directly.

Use a safe sample and the channel you agreed together. Do not put private screens or founder ideas into a public evidence link. Feedback should describe work that was actually reviewed.

Open the founder review desk →

06 · Contributor

Turn the experience into an honest work story.

Import and review the feedback note in the matching notebook record. Record your actual contribution, result, lessons, and collaborator credits. Mark the work completed and separately confirm permission before previewing a portfolio summary.

Check that the summary stays within the agreed portfolio terms. It is self-reported; received notes do not verify identity or work. Thank your collaborator and ask whether they would like to keep in touch.

Record feedback and portfolio credit →

Keep the relationship going.

A useful first contribution can lead to another conversation, a new skill, or a trusted collaborator. Save your notes and a private backup if you want to return later. The notebook and founder desk stay on this device; they do not sync or deliver messages.
