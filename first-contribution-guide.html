How it works

One contribution.
Progress for both of you.

Founders bring a direction. Builders bring strengths and curiosity. Your workspace keeps the agreement, work and feedback together.

Open my workspaceSee a 2-minute example

Real collaboration requires an approved account. The example uses fictional work.

01 · Find the fit

Start with a project or your strengths.

Founders: describe the project and define one useful contribution, with an outcome, skills, time, support and compensation. Keep private context private; choose what others can discover.

Builders: add your skills, learning goal and contribution time. Explore opportunities and why they fit, then share how you would approach the work.

Explore contributions

02 · Agree together

A clear scope before anyone starts.

Discuss questions in the contribution conversation. The founder proposes the exact deliverable, completion criteria, time, support, ownership, compensation and feedback timing. The builder reviews and explicitly confirms those terms.

Each opportunity is Paid or Voluntary. For paid work, agree the amount, currency and payment timing directly. An invitation, application or suggested match is not an agreement.

Founders & Builders takes no transaction commission and does not process or guarantee payments or resolve payment disputes. Feedback and credit do not replace agreed payment.

03 · Build and share the work

One next action. A shared record.

The builder delivers the agreed work in the workspace. Use your contribution conversation for questions, and agree on changes before extending the scope. The next-step card tells each person what needs attention or who you are waiting for.

The founder reviews the delivery and gives specific feedback. Both people can review the collaboration and suggest how to work better together.

04 · Keep growing

Take the learning—and approved credit—with you.

Your contribution record brings together the scope, delivery and feedback. Portfolio credit needs a separate explicit approval. Export only the permitted text; private messages and participant reviews stay private. Recording completion does not verify payment.

Thank your collaborator. Choose another useful contribution or a next project milestone. A working relationship can keep growing after the first task.
