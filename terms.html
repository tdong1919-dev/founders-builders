Private-beta participation.

Practical expectations for using Founders & Builders. Updated November 10, 2026.

Agree on work directly

Participants are responsible for agreeing scope, support, time, compensation and ownership before work starts. An invitation, suggestion or application does not create an agreement.

Compensation

Founders & Builders does not set rates, process payments, guarantee payment or resolve payment disputes. Portfolio credit and feedback do not replace an agreed payment.

Respect permissions

Share only work and information you have permission to share. Portfolio publication requires separate approval. Do not use agents to bypass another person’s access limits or decisions.

Understand the beta

Availability and features may change. Matching is a suggestion, not verification, endorsement or a promise of work. Fictional examples are not evidence of personal experience or real opportunities.

Before wider release

This participation guide is not a substitute for finalized operator terms. The operator’s legal identity, contact details and complete public terms require review before opening the beta to the public.
