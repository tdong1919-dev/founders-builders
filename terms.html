Practical expectations for using Founders & Builders. Updated November 10, 2026.

Agree on work directly

Participants are responsible for agreeing scope, support, time, compensation and ownership before work starts. An invitation, suggestion or application does not create an agreement.

Compensation

Founders & Builders takes no transaction commission and does not set rates, collect, hold or process payments, guarantee payment or resolve payment disputes. Participants arrange and pay for work directly. Portfolio credit and feedback do not replace an agreed payment.

Respect permissions

Share only work and information you have permission to share. Portfolio publication requires separate approval. Do not use agents to bypass another person’s access limits or decisions.

Understand the beta

Availability and features may change. Matching is a suggestion, not verification, endorsement or a promise of work. Fictional examples are not evidence of personal experience or real opportunities.

Operator and beta access

Founders & Builders is operated by JIDOKA Group. Contact support@getjidoka.com for support, privacy or account requests. Access remains approval-based; creating a profile is free. Optional future paid add-ons require a separate offer and acceptance.

Your work and account

You retain rights in content you submit, subject to the ownership terms you agree directly with collaborators. You permit the platform to store and display that content only as needed for your chosen sharing and collaboration settings. Do not upload material you cannot lawfully share. The beta is for adults aged 18 or over. You may stop participating and request deletion through the privacy contact.

Safety and availability

We may pause accounts or remove discovery cards for spam, abuse, privacy violations or misuse of agent permissions. Reports are reviewed by the operator, not automatically accepted as proof. You can ask for a review by email. The beta may be interrupted or changed; keep your own copies of important work. The platform is not a party to compensation agreements and does not guarantee collaborators, results or payments.
