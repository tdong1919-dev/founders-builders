02 · Plan, connect, reflect

Your contribution notebook.

Plan a small commitment, draft an introduction, and record what actually happened. Saving is optional and stays in this browser on this device. Use non-confidential details; browser storage is not a private shared workspace.

Work you are building on

Records do not sync across devices. Copy your work before clearing browser data.

Back up or restore my notebook

Backups include saved plans, personal notes and received sources. Keep your copy somewhere private; it is not a public portfolio. Unsaved edits are not included. Nothing is uploaded or synced.

Existing records and your open form stay unchanged. Identical copies are skipped. Restored records require fresh portfolio sharing permission; source notes and progress remain unverified.
