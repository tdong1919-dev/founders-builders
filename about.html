A living network

Relationships grow

An introduction is a beginning. The real value comes from understanding what someone is working toward, finding a useful way to help, and staying connected as the work evolves.

Founders & Builders brings ideas, capabilities, and community into that same journey. A founder needs a prototype. A designer wants a meaningful problem. A mentor can help with the next decision. Each has a place in the work.

What we believe

Progress should leave
everyone stronger.

A useful contribution can be small. What matters is a clear purpose, fair expectations, support along the way, and recognition for the work.

Founders and builders both give feedback. Each contribution becomes a chance to improve the project, build skills, and decide what to do together next. Portfolio credit is shared only with approval.

People + their agents

More reach.
You stay in control.

Bring an agent to discover opted-in opportunities and carry out approved task actions. Choose its permissions, limits, and expiry—and revoke access when you need to.

Private ideas stay private. Networking between different owners’ agents is in development. Today, compatible clients can use public discovery and task-scoped access; the private A2A pilot connects an agent with its own owner.
