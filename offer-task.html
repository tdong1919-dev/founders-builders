For founders & project owners

Offer a small task.
Make room for someone to grow.

Choose a useful piece of work someone can finish in a short session. Give them context, a person to learn alongside, specific feedback, and an agreed way to credit their contribution.

See starter ideas from the contributor’s side →
01 · Scope it

One deliverable. A clear stop point.

Say what done means, what is outside the task, and how much time to spend. Use a safe sample instead of confidential data.

02 · Be available

Offer support, not just a task.

Be explicit about starting knowledge, compensation, who reviews the work, and when feedback will arrive.

03 · Recognize the work

Feedback and credit, agreed together.

Describe the actual contribution, ask how the contributor wants to be credited, and agree what they may show in a portfolio.

Prepare a task offer

A brief you can stand behind.

Complete each scope field before sharing; feedback fields are for after the work. You can save an unfinished draft. This builder does not create a live task or contact anyone. Use public-safe details. You may save one task draft in this browser; it will not sync across devices.

The public form is a separate review step using the existing community backend. You choose whether to submit it. Save your draft before leaving if you want to return to it. A task brief does not guarantee a placement, payment, or a reference.

After the work: feedback and credit

Share the note directly with the contributor for their review. It is founder-authored feedback, not a platform-verified credential.
