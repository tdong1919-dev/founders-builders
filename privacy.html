Privacy in the private beta.

Understand what you share, where it is saved, and how agent access works. Updated November 10, 2026.

Private collaboration

Sign-in and cloud collaboration use Supabase. Sign-in email delivery uses Resend. Project briefs, participant messages and reviews are available only through authorized account access. Technical providers process data needed to run these functions.

You choose discovery

Sharing a project step with approved beta members is different from publishing a public discovery card. Public cards contain only explicitly approved summary fields. Never put confidential ideas or someone else’s private contact details in public listings.

Community records and earlier tools

Community directories use the existing Google Sheets / Apps Script integration where configured. Earlier device-only tools keep notes in browser storage; those notes are not authenticated cloud records. Some review-site directories use clearly labeled samples and do not submit to Sheets.

Agent permissions

Limited credentials authorize only selected actions within request, task-time and expiry limits. Revoking access blocks future requests, but cannot recall information already read or copied. Do not share credentials in chat, public posts or URLs.

Access requests and retention

The beta request form stores your email, chosen role and consent for manual access review. It is not a marketing subscription and does not create an account automatically. Pending beta requests are retained for up to 90 days. Account and collaboration records remain while needed for the service; deletion requests are reviewed for participant records, security and dispute obligations. Withdrawn public cards stop being discoverable, but we cannot recall copies others already made.

Your choices

You can withdraw your public discovery cards and revoke agent access in your workspace. Sign out on shared devices. Keep any exports private. For access, correction, export or deletion, email support@getjidoka.com from your account address. Do not send passwords or agent credentials. We verify ownership before action, acknowledge requests within 7 days and aim to complete deletion within 30 days. We explain any necessary exceptions or delay.

Questions and concerns

Report a listing or content concern. Founders & Builders is operated by JIDOKA Group. Support and privacy contact: support@getjidoka.com. JIDOKA’s other products and databases are separate.

Email updates and service records

Optional collaboration email updates are off by default and separate from sign-in emails and marketing. Emails contain a generic prompt to open your workspace, without private project titles, ideas, messages or delivered work. You can disable updates in your workspace or unsubscribe from an email. Security and operational records are restricted to operating the service; we do not use private collaborations as public testimonials or train an ecosystem model from them.
