Your questions, answered.

Clear answers about joining, contributions, privacy and agents. Updated November 10, 2026.

What is Founders & Builders?

Founders & Builders is a living network where people with ideas and people with complementary skills turn clear contributions into shared progress, working relationships and experience.

Who can take part?

Founders, builders and anyone growing their skills can take part. You can bring a project, contribute to someone else’s, or do both. Real collaboration currently requires approved private-beta access.

How do I get started?

Request beta access, or sign in if already invited. Then create a private project or a builder profile. You can try a fictional example without an account.

How does matching work?

Matches use shared skills, the contribution time limit and paid-work preference. Learning goals provide context. Matching does not verify ability, guarantee success or automatically commit either person.

Can beginners contribute?

Yes. Look for clearly bounded work with suitable starting knowledge and support. Discuss your experience honestly and agree on an achievable outcome before beginning.

Who handles compensation?

Founders and builders agree compensation directly. Founders & Builders does not set rates, process payments, guarantee payment or resolve payment disputes. Feedback and portfolio credit are separate.

What is public and what stays private?

Private project ideas, participant conversations and reviews are not public discovery content. Sharing with approved beta members and publishing a public discovery card are separate choices. Public community listings may contain contact details; review them before submitting.

Can my agent use the platform?

Compatible agents can read opted-in public discovery cards. Owner-issued, task-scoped credentials allow specific approved actions with request, time and expiry limits. People confirm agreements, reviews and portfolio permissions. See supported connection options.

Do agents network autonomously with other owners’ agents?

Cross-owner agent networking is in development. The private A2A pilot connects an agent with its own owner. Agents run in their own apps; the platform enforces the access their owner grants.

Are contributions and reviews public?

Collaboration reviews stay between participants. Portfolio text requires separate permission. Public discovery cards contain only the summary their owner explicitly approves.

Is every page a live workspace?

No. Examples use fictional data. Earlier tools keep device-only records separate. Your workspace is the primary place for authenticated collaboration.

What do founders and builders get here?

Founders get direction, support and community to move an idea forward. Builders gain experience, relationships tied to real work, and feedback and approved credit for a resume or portfolio. Paid opportunities can also offer income; no work or earnings are guaranteed.

Does the platform take a cut of payments?

No transaction commission. Participants agree and arrange payments directly. Founders & Builders does not collect, hold, process or guarantee payments or resolve payment disputes. Agree the amount, timing and deliverable before starting paid work.

Will membership or promotion cost money?

Joining the approval-based beta is currently free. Future membership fees and optional sponsored or boosted placements are planned; prices and a membership milestone have not been announced. There is no checkout or automatic charge. Any future paid offer will state its price and terms before you choose it. Sponsored visibility will be clearly labeled and will not mean endorsement, verified skills or a guaranteed match.
