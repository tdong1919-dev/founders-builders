# Contribution stories: private preparation and publication

The workspace offers a private draft only after a contribution is closed, delivered and confirmed under a recorded terms version. Drafts are prepared in the current page, without transmission, persistence or publication. Fictional previews and nonparticipants are rejected. Private conversations and participant feedback are not copied into the draft. The draft can itself contain confidential scope or delivery text: review and redact before sharing it for approval.

No public contribution stories or testimonials are included in this release. The operator has not recorded both participants' permission to publish a real story. Do not use test records as customer proof.

Before publishing any future story:

1. Verify it concerns actual work, not a demo or account test.
2. Agree on a public-safe draft, the chosen names or aliases and permissible evidence. Avoid private ideas, contact details, messages or reviews.
3. Obtain and keep each participant's approval of the exact final text, names, evidence, destination and intended marketing use. Portfolio permission is not marketing permission.
4. Check that outcomes and metrics are supported by the records or authorized evidence. Do not imply verified payment, guaranteed results or independently verified skill.
5. Publish only the approved version. Contact participants again for material changes. Handle withdrawal requests through support@getjidoka.com and explain that already copied material cannot be recalled.

This editorial process does not grant access, change agreements, certify legal sufficiency or create a public story automatically.
