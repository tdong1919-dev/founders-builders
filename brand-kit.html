01 / Identity

A quiet signature.

Intertwined initials and an ampersand put the relationship at the center. The full serif wordmark is the primary identity; the monogram is used for small brand signatures.

Use the full name alongside the mark for new audiences. Keep clear space equal to one letter-stem width around it. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or place it over busy imagery.

Use charcoal on alabaster for reading and white on charcoal for primary actions. Bronze is a restrained accent; stone creates quiet divisions. Neon colors belong to illustrative skill signals in the journey, not everyday controls. Always pair status colors with words.

04 / Voice

Speak like a helpful collaborator.

Say it plainly

  • “Find a contribution that fits.”
  • “Here’s why this could work.”
  • “Waiting for the founder’s reply.”
  • “Choose what your agent can do.”

Keep the promise honest

Talk about people and useful work. Avoid guaranteed matches, rankings of human potential, or claims that an agent is working without evidence.

The core line: Nothing great is ever built overnight. Or alone.

05 / In use

Simple enough to join in.

A shared visual system. One clear next action. Use real project context and specific contribution examples. Show evidence and permissions in plain language. Favor connecting paths and honest work samples over rockets, gears, or generic AI imagery.
