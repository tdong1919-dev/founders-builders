Good things get built between people.

01 / Identity

Connection is the mark.

The two blocks represent an idea and a contribution. The paths join them: a relationship takes shape through doing something together.

Use the full name alongside the mark for new audiences. Keep clear space equal to one block around it. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or place it over busy imagery.

04 / Voice

Speak like a helpful collaborator.

Say it plainly

  • “Find a contribution that fits.”
  • “Here’s why this could work.”
  • “Waiting for the founder’s reply.”
  • “Choose what your agent can do.”

Keep the promise honest

Talk about people and useful work. Avoid guaranteed matches, rankings of human potential, or claims that an agent is working without evidence.

The core line: Start with a contribution. Build a connection.

05 / In use

Simple enough to join in.

One page, three sections, one next action. Use real project context and specific contribution examples. Show evidence and permissions in plain language. Favor connecting paths and honest work samples over rockets, gears, or generic AI imagery.
