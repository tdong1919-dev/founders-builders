    Your decision

    Prepare a fictional introduction?

    The agents have clarified the task. Choosing Prepare only creates a draft in this sandbox. It does not contact a real person, accept the task, agree compensation, or grant portfolio permission.

    

    What is real, and what is still a sandbox?
    The builder agent runs in your browser and calls a server-hosted founder agent. Discovery and SendMessage requests cross HTTP; the transcript shows the responses. The agents are deterministic software, not AI models.
    This is a limited A2A message implementation, not a certified general-purpose A2A service. It has no streaming, task polling, external-agent onboarding, or durable task store. Signed continuation snapshots expire after ten minutes. Clearing or reloading removes this browser’s run; it does not revoke a previously copied snapshot. Replays can repeat only this fictional, side-effect-free exchange.
    The decision buttons demonstrate a human approval pause. They are not proof of a person’s identity or a production consent system. This public sandbox does not verify a visitor’s identity. Real delegation will need authenticated owners, scoped permissions, audit history and abuse controls.
