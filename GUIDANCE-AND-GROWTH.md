# Direction and growth in the existing workspace

## Founder next-step guidance

Owned project overviews have an optional Help me choose the next step disclosure. The saved milestone anchors three editable starting points: learn from a question, review an experience, or explore a small prototype. These are local scaffolds, not an AI feasibility assessment. The founder reviews three draft fields and checks a separate acknowledgment before using the scaffold. Editing invalidates acknowledgment; detached, refreshed and signed-out controls are inert.

Using a starting point only fills empty contribution fields. It keeps existing text and payment terms, refuses a conflicting project draft before changing anything, and clears public sharing when it adds draft content. Nothing is saved, sent, agreed, granted or published. The normal task form still requires the founder to review scope, time, support, skills, compensation, ownership and sharing before saving.

## Builder experience summary

Builder view groups completed contributions with actual recorded delivery notes. It separates delivered work, current task tags, private feedback, and approved/recorded portfolio credit. Applications and active/cancelled work are not counted as completed experience. The fictional preview remains labeled as fictional; its sample progress is not evidence of real experience. Closed records without delivery notes are excluded from the experience count. Task tags are current metadata, not independently verified skills or evidence that the builder practiced every listed skill. No earnings or payment status is inferred.

Private feedback is not portfolio permission. Only separately approved and recorded credit appears as portfolio text. Revocation removes that text on fresh data. Review contribution and credit opens the exact existing record, where export is still checked by the server. No new export, automatic resume claims, public profile publication, external service or storage is introduced. Missing task targets remain read-only references rather than opening another task.

## Evidence-based improvement

One improvement to try presents at most three suggestions from authorized records: a handoff without a recorded update for at least seven days, a real counterpart's private review, a missing personal review after recorded delivery, or missing details in an owned, unpaused task. A completed delivery can also anchor reflection when no more specific signal exists. Each suggestion identifies its source and opens the exact record without writing. Unknown and future update times are not classified as inactivity; inactivity is not proof that work stopped. No ecosystem ranking, inferred success score or automatic outreach is claimed.

## Validation and release scope

Nine new tests exercise owned milestone derivation, completed-work selection, credit permission/revocation, evidence/unknown dates, missing targets, review invalidation and detached controls, atomic draft preservation, literal rendering, and integration with the actual workspace controller and collaboration rules. Full regression: 492 tests pass. All 66 static HTML pages pass local assets/links/anchors/unique-ID checks.

Frontend only. No database, authentication, backend, agent permission, billing, Resend, Sheets, migration or cost changes. Email activation and the real desktop/mobile/two-account walkthrough remain deferred by the user. Changes extend PR #62 rather than adding another stack.
