Turn feedback into a test

Can a clearer brief save a question?

Compare the same fictional task with different levels of detail. Both agents keep exactly the same permissions and limits.

This is a controlled rule-based example, not evidence about real people or ecosystem success. Both runs stop at a proposal ready for review. No introduction is prepared or sent.

Back to the orchestrator report →

What changes?

Incomplete brief

“Help review our onboarding.”

The agent needs a scope question before it can propose the task.

Clearer brief

“Write three observations on one sample onboarding screen, within 45 minutes. A 15-minute founder check-in and feedback within three days are available.”

The scope is ready to review. Compensation, ownership, credit and portfolio permission still require discussion before real work.

Keep the boundaries fixed

These are simulated settings, not authenticated owner permissions. Time limits apply to both briefs. With questions disabled, a brief may still reach review if it needs no clarification; no forbidden question is sent.
