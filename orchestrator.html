      What the production orchestrator would add
      Authenticated owner policies, revocable delegation, a durable communication ledger, monitored delivery, and an owner dashboard. Participants could control transcript retention and opt into learning from de-identified outcomes. Reports would separate drop-offs, scope changes, policy stops and request errors from actual completed work and participant feedback.
      Cross-ecosystem recommendations would require sufficient comparable, consented evidence and would cite their basis. Private ideas and raw conversations would not become training or peer-comparison data by default. Suggestions could improve briefs or missions; they would never silently loosen permissions.
