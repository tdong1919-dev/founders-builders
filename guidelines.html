Build with respect.

Clear expectations make it easier to trust the next conversation.

Be specific and honest

Represent your skills, availability, project status and offers accurately. Label sample work. Never invent testimonials, completed contributions or credentials.

Make room to learn

Offer bounded tasks and realistic support. Give specific, constructive feedback on the work and the collaboration. Allow people to decline without pressure.

Protect ideas and credit

Respect confidential information and ownership terms. Ask before sharing a person’s work, name or portfolio credit. Do not copy private records into public channels or agents without authorization.

Keep outreach useful

Contact people about relevant opportunities they have chosen to make discoverable. Do not send spam or repeated messages after someone declines.

Report concerns

Report misleading content, spam or misconduct. Reports are reviewed by Crystal, the platform operator, and do not automatically remove content. Email support@getjidoka.com for safety concerns, account review or to appeal a decision. We acknowledge requests within 7 days. Do not include credentials. Reporting does not provide payment recovery. Harassment, discrimination, spam, impersonation and unauthorized sharing are prohibited.
